## 🚀 Projects

### 🚗 Tata Nano EV Conversion
Converted a Tata Nano from an Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) vehicle into a fully electric vehicle. This project involved electric drivetrain integration, battery systems, motor controllers, high-current wiring, and practical automotive electronics.

### 🏍 Custom Electric Bike
Designed and built a custom electric bike while working with BLDC motors, battery packs, motor controllers, electrical wiring, and complete system integration.

### 🔋 Custom Lithium Battery Packs
Designed and assembled lithium-ion battery packs from individual cells using Battery Management Systems (BMS). Built battery packs with different series and parallel configurations to achieve specific voltages and capacities while ensuring proper cell balancing, safety, and protection.

### ☀️ Solar Power Systems
Hands-on experience with solar power systems utilizing MPPT charge controllers, battery charging, automatic power changeover, electrical protection, and energy distribution.

### ⚙️ Electronics & Embedded Projects
Worked on numerous electronics projects involving Arduino, sensors, relays, MOSFETs, automation systems, motor control, and embedded hardware while continuously exploring new technologies.
